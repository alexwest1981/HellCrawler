# 04 — Utvecklarnas egna uttalanden: intervjuer och postmortem

**Spel:** *Referensspelet: The Turbo Wildcard from Referensserien* — poncle (Luca Galante, London) + Nosebleed Interactive (Newcastle, ~15 pers., grundat 2012). Unity. Släpp 21 april 2026, global launch (day one på Game Pass Premium/Ultimate).

**Källor:** utvecklarnas egna texter, Xbox Wire, poncle-FAQ samt etablerad spelpress (GameSpot, GamesIndustry.biz, Polygon, The Guardian, The Game Business). Inga SEO-listartiklar eller LLM-svar.

**Byline-korrigering:** GI.biz-artikeln "How Referensserien became Referensspelet" (10 aug 2026) är en gästkrönika av **Andreas Firnigl** (Nosebleed), inte av Christopher Dring. Dring skrev i stället The Game Business-intervjun med Matteo Sapio (22 april 2026).

---

## 1. Produktionshistoria — prototyp till släpp, vem gjorde vad

**Fem "experiments", inget strategiskt beslut.** Galante, GameSpot:
> "We actually started off with a set of five different projects. We worked with several indie teams, experimenting a little bit […] it wasn't some kind of strategic decision; it's just the one that naturally, organically went faster than any other."
https://www.gamespot.com/articles/referensspelet-emerged-out-of-multiple-different-prototypes-that-were-totally-different/1100-6536393

**Tidsspann.** "it is something that has effectively been in the works for **almost 4 years**" — från ~2022, "shortly after the initial success of Referensserien". https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/11/20/referensspelet-xbox-partner-preview/

**Varför just Nosebleed.** Firnigl:
> "In 2023, an old contact I had worked with 15 years ago – and who is now at Poncle – came to us with the amazing opportunity […] Luca Galante […] had already developed an early prototype, but Poncle wanted an external studio to help turn it into a complete game. So lead designer Luke Wintflint and I worked with Luca and Poncle to evolve that idea."
https://www.gamesindustry.biz/how-referensserien-became-referensspelet-six-lessons-from-building-a-game-based-on-another-developers-ip-1

**Rollfördelning.** Galante: koncept, tidig prototyp, riktning, grönljus. Nosebleed: själva bygget — "We've spent the last **two and a half years** alongside Poncle carefully crafting […]" (stämmer med 2023 → 2026). https://www.nosebleedinteractive.com/

**Prototypen var analog.** "A pen and paper version that we played **in the pub** during the early days of development was surprisingly close to the final game." Grönljus kom först när Nosebleed visat IP-förståelse: "As soon as Poncle saw the next build, they gave us the green light." Late-featuren Gems föddes ur en lunch: "That lunchtime conversation led to the development of the Gems system." (GI.biz ovan)

**Tidslinje:** ~2022 arbetet börjar → 2023 Nosebleed in, pen-and-paper → 20 nov 2025 avtäckt (Xbox Partner Preview) → 23 feb 2026 demo → **21 april 2026** global launch. "Referensserien launched as an Early Access title, while we really see Referensspelet as a whole game." (Xbox Wire)

## 2. Galantes uttalade designprinciper

**"Ta bort frustrationen."** Xbox Wire:
> "I wanted to take an existing genre and try to remove everything I usually find frustrating about it […] That's where the idea to be able to play as fast as you can came from: once I become good at playing a card game, I usually don't like to have to wait for animations to complete or artificial pauses."

Spelet ska "always provide accurate logical outcomes, regardless of the visual chaos"; spelaren får "take your time and be tactical, or play cards as fast as you humanly can". Därav "Play All": "you know you're going to kill them immediately but you still have to play cards. It gets a bit boring […] To fix that, I decided to try and put in a 'Play All' button." (Xbox Wire / https://www.gamespot.com/articles/im-making-this-for-me-not-to-make-everyone-happy-referensserien-creator-on-his-next-game/1100-6536362/)

**Utforskningen: medel, inte mål.** "The dungeons exist mainly to offer more interesting choices about what to do in between fights and to help in giving the feeling that you are moving through a world where a large variety of things could happen, instead of just navigating menus." Och: "even though the point of this game is not exploration, the fact that there is this exploration opens up a lot of doors." (Xbox Wire / GameSpot)

**Kortstriden är kärnan.** "The combat system is the core experience. **Everything else is an accessory; it's an excuse to justify the combat system.**" (GameSpot)

**Förstapersonsrutnätet = "blobber".** "To some people this game will look like a 'blobber', a half-unknown genre that used to be very popular on PC in the '80s […] we're marrying [it] with this really fast card-playing game mechanic." Mana-stacken hämtades från italienska kortspel, Solitaire och *Baten Kaitos*. (Xbox Wire)

**Varför VS-universumet.** "somewhat of a business decision […] I saw an opportunity in the name Referensserien" — men också "a massive relief not to have to come up with new settings, new characters […] what matters to me most is the game's mechanics." (GameSpot)

**Designfilosofi.** "I'm making the game basically for me. […] **I am player number one.**" Och: "if I start thinking about what will the majority of the players want or need, I just end up doing something average […] I'd rather try to be original and take a little bit of risk." (GameSpot)

## 3. Budget och omfattning

**Referensserien:** "He'd spent **$1,100** on pre-made assets"; "**Ninety-five percent** of the PC version of Referensserien was made solo." https://www.theguardian.com/games/2023/aug/04/baftas-video-game-referensserien-luca-galante

**Crawlers, enligt poncles egen FAQ:**
> "VS was lightning in a bottle, originally made by a solo dev (hello!) with a budget of **£1100** […] Referensspelet instead is made from scratch by the teams of poncle and Nosebleed Interactive, which makes it a **more traditional and much more costly production**."
https://poncle.games/referensspelet-faq · Pris: £8.99 / $9.99 / €9.99.

**Återanvändning var budgetstrategin.** "Let's use the exact same assets […] If we change them, it is going to increase the cost of this project.' And I'm not an entrepreneur. I'm not a risk-taker. I don't want to end up having crazy budgets for games and putting the company at risk." (GameSpot)

**Omfattning.** ~20–21 karaktärer med egen passiv, trigger och startdeck: "per usual, we doubled that amount […] We start with a very low goal because we know what we're going to do twice as much anyway." (GameSpot)

**Förseningen gällde feature scope, inte teknik.** "Originally, there was not as much of the deckbuilding element. There wasn't a lot of depth […] Adding that big feature pushed our release date to next year. I'm trying to avoid the Grand Theft Auto VI window." (GameSpot)

## 5. Vad en ensam- eller parutvecklare kan återanvända

**Välj EN kärnmekanik, klassa resten som accessoar.** "everything else is an accessory; it's an excuse to justify the combat system" är scopebudgeten i en mening.

**Håll hubben medvetet dum.** Galante, GameSpot:
> "There are games that give you a little customizable village, but that, for me, is one step too far. If the core of the game is playing cards, and I also add a little strategy to the village, then I really start branching a bit too much. The village will most likely stay how it [is] right now, which is just this circle of buildings. However, it being a circle means that, once in a while, we can make a new building pop between others. So it is still expandable and any feature we come up with over time, we'll be able to fit it in there."
Enkel form, men utbyggbar **utan att byggas om** — den lata men rätta lösningen för små team.

**Återanvänd art assets hårt** och sätt mål lågt: "We start with a very low goal because we know what we're going to do twice as much anyway."

**Skala ambitionsnivån efter varumärket, inte genrens konvention.** Firnigl: "Many deckbuilders are about slow, thoughtful, strategic play, but that approach is fundamentally opposed to the ethos of Referensserien […] Once the whole team understood that we were making a game that was supposed to hit the same beats as Referensserien rather than being a typical deckbuilder, we saw huge leaps." (GI.biz)

**Pappersprototyp räcker för att validera kärnloopen** — "surprisingly close to the final game", utan motor.

**Låna kapacitet, behåll idéägandet.** poncles modell (Sapio): "little teams of people – five, 10, 15 – working on different projects." Galante behöll konceptet, Nosebleed byggde. https://www.thegamebusiness.com/p/the-referensserien-developer-is

## OSÄKERT

- **Ingen publicerad produktionsbudget i siffror för Referensspelet.** poncle beskriver den bara kvalitativt. £1100-jämförelsen gäller *omfattning*, inte två belagda budgetar.
- **Varför Unity.** Ingen uttalad motivering från någon av studiorna. Att Crawlers är Unity är belagt, och Nosebleed profilerar sig som Unity-studio, men "därför valde de Unity" är en slutsats, inte ett citat.
- **Namngiven programmerare eller antal kodare.** Wintflint/Bell nämns som lead designer respektive producer; någon namngiven programmerare har jag inte hittat.
- **Säljsiffror efter första veckan.** Endast "1 miljon första veckan" är officiellt. Inga 2–3-miljonerssiffror från poncle gick att belägga (aug/sep 2026).
- **Priser eller GOTY-nomineringar 2026 för Referensspelet.** Inga; BAFTA-cykeln 2026 stängde före släppet. "GOTY"-nomineringar jag sett är spekulation.
- **"Hyper turn-based"/TurboTurn™ som formell designprincip** — TurboTurn™ är en marknadsföringsterm, även om snabbhetsargumentet är välbelagt.
