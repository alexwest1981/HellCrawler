# Crawlers:Relics

Jump to navigation Jump to search
**Relics** in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)* are unique treasure found hidden in various [dungeons](/w/Crawlers:Dungeons). They unlock new mechanics in the game.

Although the concept of relics was introduced in the demo, with the inclusion of the [![](/images/CSprite-Combo_Stack.png?a4216)](/w/Crawlers:Combo_Stack) [Combo Stack](/w/Crawlers:Combo_Stack), the [![](/images/CSprite-Gem_Hammer.png?a4216)](/w/Crawlers:Gem_Hammer) [Gem Hammer](/w/Crawlers:Gem_Hammer), and the [![](/images/CSprite-Guiding_Light.png?a4216)](/w/Crawlers:Guiding_Light) [Guiding Light](/w/Crawlers:Guiding_Light), the ability to browse all collected relics in the [Museum](/w/Crawlers:Museum) was introduced in the full game's release.
