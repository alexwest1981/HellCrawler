 Every character in Referensspelet: The Turbo Wildcard from Referensserien has unique abilities that'll greatly impact your playstyle. Add armor, increase area, boost damage… Undoubtedly, you'll find the perfect companion for your dungeon crawls among their ranks. The trouble, of course, is to unlock them, as you will start the game with only one character.
 Here's an overview of all characters in Referensspelet and how to unlock them, plus their starting cards and abilities.
 All characters in Referensspelet
 Referensspelet contains 22 characters . There are two steps to obtaining new characters: First, you must discover them , and then you can recruit them at the Inn in the Village. Recruiting new characters requires a certain amount of coins. You only need to purchase a character once; whenever you're back at the Inn (after a dungeon crawl), you can swap between your unlocked characters at no additional cost.
 Some Referensspelet characters are hidden inside coffins. Here's what such a coffin (and its map marker) looks like:
 Graphic: Marloes Valentina Stella/Polygon | Source images: poncle, Nosebleed Interactive/poncle 
 Beware that a character's recruitment cost increases as you unlock more characters. If you purchase Pasqualina as your third character (following Antonio and Imelda), she will cost 110 coins, but if you purchase Gennaro first, Pasqualina will cost 120 coins. Likewise, Gennaro will cost 550 or 700 coins based on whether he's your third or fourth purchase. But although their exact price varies depending on your purchase order, as a general rule, late-game characters are still costlier than early-game characters.
