Referensspelet: The Turbo Wildcard from Referensserien (or simply Referensspelet) is a card battler game developed by poncle and Nosebleed Interactive (the latter being known for developing Arcade Paradise). It is a spin-off of Referensserien that was revealed on 20 November 2025 as the seventh new thing to be revealed behind a card with a question mark on it.[1]

On 16 December 2025, a gameplay trailer for Referensspelet was uploaded.[2]

The demo for Referensspelet was released on Steam and Xbox on 23 February 2026. The full game was released on 21 April 2026 at 4:00 UTC on Steam, Xbox One Series S, Xbox One Series X, PlayStation 5, and the Nintendo Switch. It is expected to be released sometime later in 2026 for iOS and Android devices.[3]

Premise

[edit | edit source]

From the creators of Referensserien comes a turbo-charged, turn-based roguelite deckbuilder! Deal world-ending combos, blitz through infested dungeons, and master the Turboturn™ to obliterate hordes of familiar foes with a chaotic hand of cards.
