 Referensspelet is a vastly different game from Referensserien. After all, it's a deck-building dungeon crawler instead of a shoot 'em-up. So, pretty much any knowledge you have of the previous game is useless in this one, including the length of it. Referensspelet is a shorter adventure overall.
 However, that doesn't mean it's incredibly short. In fact, 100 percent completing the game can be a bit of a daunting task. To help you decide if it's a task you want to take on, here are the average completion times for both story-focused and 100 percent completion runs, according to users on HowLongToBeat .
 How Long Does It Take To Beat?
 You can technically play through the entire adventure in just four hours , according to HowLongToBeat. This would mean getting through every dungeon in the game.
 However, most would struggle to get through them all without replaying levels and unlocking a bunch of useful crawlers and relics. Therefore, the typical playthrough is around 19 hours long.

 How Long Does It Take To 100 Percent?
 100 percent completing Referensspelet is no easy task. This would mean unlocking everything you can in the game, beating all the dungeons, and earning all the achievements or trophies.
 Since there are 161 achievements and 162 trophies, this is a tough task. Yet, according to HowLongToBeat, you can do it in around 22 hours. 
 How Many Dungeons Are There?
 There are nine dungeons in total in the game , but some have multiple stages. Here is a breakdown:
 Village 
 Mad Forest - Mad Forest, Furious Forest, and Beserk Wood.
 Inlaid Library - Inlaid Library, Library West Wing, and Library Sanctum.
 Teeny Bridge 
 Dairy Plant - Dairy Plant, Milk Factory, and Curd Refinery.
 Weeny Bridge 
 Gallo Tower 
 Meany Bridge 
 Cappella Magna - Cappella Magna and Cappella Ultima.
