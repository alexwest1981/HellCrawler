**Characters** or **Crawlers** are the character cards in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)*. Players can purchase and equip characters through the [Gorton Bell Inn](/w/Crawlers:Gorton_Bell_Inn).

The leading main (first chosen) crawler adds their own parametres / stats / power ups to the party total (E.G. [Antonio](/w/Crawlers:Antonio) adds +10 [Max Health](/w/Crawlers:Max_Health)). The leading main crawler also adds four (cards) to the starter deck. Any following crawler (from Gorton Bell and [recruited within dungeons](/w/Crawlers:Outhouse)) adds no additional power ups, and only one of their attack / spell cards is added to the starter deck (E.G. non-leading [Poppea](/w/Crawlers:Poppea) adds only one [Song of Mana](/w/Crawlers:Song_of_Mana) to the starter deck and no other cards).

Each character has their own unique Crawler effect on their card, which is typically triggered by playing a particular color card, and lasts for a set amount, increased by the [![](/images/CSprite-Duration.png?3692c)](/w/Crawlers:Duration) [Duration](/w/Crawlers:Duration) deck stat. A higher Duration stat adds increases the initial amount to the character's Crawler effect trigger amount.

Once 5 Crawlers have been purchased, there is a 10% chance to trigger a "Disco mode" in the Inn. During Disco mode, a 5% discount is applied to additional purchases of Crawlers. The Disco mode ends when leaving the Inn. And requires a dungeon run in order to be eligible for a chance of re-activation.
