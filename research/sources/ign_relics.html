Relic Guide

By Tenkiei, Meg Koepp, +3 more
Updated: May 1, 2026 5:57pm UTC
Add IGN on Google

Relics are special items you can find in Referensspelet. Each time you obtain a new Relic, you unlock new game mechanics. The first of these is the Combo Stack, which you find during the tutorial. After you play two dungeons, you unlock the Relic Museum, which shows all of your Relics and allows you to toggle a Relic's effect off. This Referensspelet guide teaches you everything you need to know about Relics, including how to unlock them all.

Relics Explained

A few of your Relics (Rivelatore, Milky Way Map, and Combo Stack) are active and provide baseline game effects. If you continue exploring, you unlock Gems with the Gem Hammer, Arcana via the Polentir (with the Arcana Finder they can show up in random dungeon chests), the Blacksmith with the Stardust Anvil, and the Jeweler through the Lapidary Loop.

Meanwhile the Guiding Light and Grim Grimoire give you access to more information.

Bomba Infernale

The Bomba Infernale unlocks a big red button, appearing on the first floor of a dungeon. When you press it, it instantly vaporizes everything on the map's first level. This is a good way to get a bunch of XP (and cards) to head to the next floor. Though once you unlock Ovenkilt, you generally get less gold as you don't get to overkill the vaporized enemies.

Sorceress' Tears (Hurry)

The Sorceress' Tears add a Hurry button to the world map. This speeds your animations up both on the world map and within a dungeon. This is particularly helpful if you have a high Amount stat as it can take a while for weapons to fire all of their projectiles.

Overkill

Overkill is a mechanic that earns you extra coins in exchange for doing gratuitous damage to enemies. It's tied to the Overkill, Ultimate Ultra Overkill, and Ovenkilt Relics.

  • By default, you can overkill bosses to earn up to 500 coins.
  • With Ultimate Ultra Overkill, you can earn up to 5,000, which caps the mechanic at "Ultra Maximum Overkill."
  • Ovenkilt extends the overkill fun to overkill any enemy and earn up to 5,000 coins.

You get Ovenkilt by completing Capella Ultima, which is the difficulty 9 version of Capella Magna. So if you want to farm lots of gold, beat the game, then equip Gallo (for his greed bonus), and try to get a Midas Gem. Doing this yields hundreds of thousands to millions of Coins per run.
