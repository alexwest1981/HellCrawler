# Crawlers : Wild cards
**Wild cards** are grey-colored cards in _Referensspelet_ which can be used at any point without breaking a Combo. Most are single-use cards that are destroyed after, and do not scale with the combo multiplier.
Their primary benefit is that the next card will continue the combo regardless of cost, letting the player combo up another chain of consecutive costing cards.
