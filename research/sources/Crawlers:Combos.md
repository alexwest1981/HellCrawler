# Crawlers:Combo

From Referensserien Wiki

(Redirected from [Crawlers:Combos](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combos?redirect=no "Crawlers:Combos"))

[Jump to navigation](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo#searchInput)

Combo is the core mechanism in Referensspelet.

It is available after obtaining [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Combo_Stack.png?a4216)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo_Stack "Combo Stack")[Combo Stack](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo_Stack "Crawlers:Combo Stack").

Playing cards in ascending order of mana cost alongside [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Wild.png/24px-CSprite-Wild.png?0f4db)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Wild "Wild")[Wild](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Wild "Crawlers:Wild") cards increase the combo multiplier stack.

- For an XX‑combo, the effect is equivalent to playing the card XX additional times repeatedly. Combo only receives benefits from [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Might.png?94fbc)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Might "Might")[Might](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Might "Crawlers:Might"), [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Area.png?4f96a)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Area "Area")[Area](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Area "Crawlers:Area"), [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Double_Damage.png/24px-CSprite-Double_Damage.png?7bb3b)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Double_Damage "Double Damage")[Double Damage](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Double_Damage "Crawlers:Double Damage"), [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Triple_Damage.png/24px-CSprite-Triple_Damage.png?3daab)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Triple_Damage "Triple Damage")[Triple Damage](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Triple_Damage "Crawlers:Triple Damage") and [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Leader.png/24px-CSprite-Leader.png?5a492)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Leader "Leader")[Leader](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Leader "Crawlers:Leader").

The combo multiplier will be displayed at the bottom‑right corner of the screen.
