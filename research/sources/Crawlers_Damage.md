# Crawlers:Damage

From Referensserien Wiki

[Jump to navigation](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Damage#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Damage#searchInput)

Damage is the number displayed on enemies when using a [card](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Cards "Crawlers:Cards"). Damage can not be higher than the enemy's health left.

[Link to this section](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Damage#Damage_calculation "Link to this section")

## Damage calculation

\[ [edit](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Damage?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Damage calculation") \| [edit source](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Damage?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Damage calculation")\]

Damage=(CardDamage+CardDamage\*Combo+BaseDamage\*Amount)\*(1+Might)\*(1+Area/5)\*GemMultiplier1\*GemMultiplier2

Damage: the number displayed on enemies.

_CardDamage_: the number displayed on [cards](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Attack_cards "Crawlers:Attack cards") while not in combat.

[Combo](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo "Crawlers:Combo"): the multiplier number when stacking combos.

[![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Amount.png?2539a)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Amount")[Amount](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Crawlers:Amount"): capped at 50.

[![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Might.png?94fbc)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Might "Might")[Might](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Might "Crawlers:Might"): capped at 1000%.

[![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Area.png?4f96a)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Area "Area")[Area](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Area "Crawlers:Area"): capped at 1000%. Every 5% area adds 1% bonus damage to every enemy in front.

_GemMultiplier_: [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Double_Damage.png/24px-CSprite-Double_Damage.png?7bb3b)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Double_Damage "Double Damage")[Double Damage](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Double_Damage "Crawlers:Double Damage") and [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/thumb/CSprite-Triple_Damage.png/24px-CSprite-Triple_Damage.png?3daab)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Triple_Damage "Triple Damage")[Triple Damage](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Triple_Damage "Crawlers:Triple Damage") have this. Stack multiplicatively.

Since [Combo](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo "Crawlers:Combo") and [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Amount.png?2539a)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Amount")[Amount](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Crawlers:Amount") share the same multiplier bucket, [![](https://referens.survivors.wiki/images/CSprite-Amount.png?2539a)](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Amount")[Amount](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Amount "Crawlers:Amount") is valuable at low [Combo](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo "Crawlers:Combo") multiplier, while its effectiveness diminishes as [Combo](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Combo "Crawlers:Combo") multiplier stacks.
