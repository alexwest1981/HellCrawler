**RedDeath** is an [enemy](/w/Crawlers:Enemies) in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)*. It is encountered at the end of the final floor in every [dungeon](/w/Crawlers:Dungeons), and attacks first before the player draws cards. Typically this will kill the player and end the run as a successful completion of the dungeon, however, if enough max health has been obtained, this initial attack can be survived. Despite being a difficult enemy to defeat and having similar mechanics to other bosses, internally it is neither classified as a boss nor has a high difficulty value. Interacting with the final shovel again after killing a RedDeath summons another RedDeath.

In the demo, defeating RedDeath does not have any benefit or unlock.

In the full game, defeating RedDeath unlocks the [MissingN0](/w/Crawlers:MissingN0) crawler, functioning similar to the [Mask of the Red Death](/w/Mask_of_the_Red_Death) character in Referensserien. Each RedDeath defeated also adds +100% temporary [Curse](/w/Crawlers:Curse), increasing the max hit and health of subsequent RedDeaths.
