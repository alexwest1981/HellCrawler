The Blacksmith's Workshop, [1] or simply the Blacksmith, is one of the eight buildings that can be found in the Village. It has two functions: 

- It allows for adding gem slots in Blacksmith.
- It allows for modifying rarities of gems in Jeweller.

The Blacksmith's shop is unlocked after obtaining the Stardust Anvil. The Jeweller's shop is unlocked by obtaining the Lapidary Loupe.

## Jeweller

Gems are classified into five rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare, and Ultra Rare. See Gems for further details. 

Upgrading a gem's rarity beyond Ultra Rare seals the gem, rendering it unavailable from chests or level ups. 

The cost of changing gem rarity rises with each successive use and is capped at 50,000 coins.
