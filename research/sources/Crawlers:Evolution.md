**Evolution** is a [gem](/w/Crawlers:Gems) in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)*. It will only appear if the player has a specific combination of cards in their deck. In some cases, the secondary card can be any card from a group of similar cards. Both the primary and secondary card get consumed, resulting in just the evolution being added to the deck.

The primary card selected needs to have an available gem slot for the Evolution gem to be inserted into. Any remaining gems on the primary card will be inherited by the evolution, but the gems in the secondary, and tertiary, cards will be lost when the primary card is evolved.

If one attempts to earn an Evolution gem from the [evolution statue](/w/Crawlers:Evolution_statue) without having valid cards for an evolution, it will instead offer evolution fulfilment components such as a Whip for Hollow Heart or vice versa.
