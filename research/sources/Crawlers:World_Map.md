The World Map is one of the eight areas that can be found in the Village. It allows for the player to select and access a specific Dungeon. It is unlocked after completing the Tutorial. 

Players can navigate to the World Map option in the Village quicker by pressing Backspace. Pressing that key while in the World Map will lead back to the Village option within the World Map. 

If the player has the Sorceress' Tears or the Seventh Trumpet, they can enable or disable their respective modes, Hurry Mode and Endless Mode, through the World Map or the Museum.

## Trivia

- This is one of the three areas, within the Village, that are visible after completing the Tutorial, with the other being the Town Hall and the Graveyard.
- Before the 1.5 update, the player could press Esc in the Village to instantly access the World Map, and pressing it in the World Map would instantly access the Village.
