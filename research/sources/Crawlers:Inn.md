The **Gorton Bell Inn**,[1] or simply the **Inn**, is one of the eight [buildings](/w/Crawlers:Buildings) that can be found in the [Village](/w/Crawlers:Village). It allows for the player to buy and equip [Crawlers](/w/Crawlers:Crawlers).

It is unlocked by completing Floor 1 in [Mad Forest](/w/Crawlers:Mad_Forest). Doing so would also unlock the ability to purchase [Imelda](/w/Crawlers:Imelda).
