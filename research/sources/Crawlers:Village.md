**Gorton** , [[ 1 ]]() also known as the **Village** , is the main hub of _Referensspelet_ where multiple buildings for various mechanics are present. It is unlocked after completing the Tutorial .

## Navigation
The player can press W , Space , or Enter , to enter any building. While the player is in a building, the player can press Esc or Backspace [[ 2 ]]() to exit out of the building.
While in the Village and not in any building, pressing Backspace will navigate the player towards the World Map option in the Village. If the player is in the World Map, pressing that key will lead to the Village option in the World Map.
Pressing Esc while in the Village, or the World Map, will immediately lead to the Settings menu. Before the 1.5 update, doing so would lead to the World Map, and pressing it again would lead back to the Village.
