# Crawlers:Cards

Cards are the core mechanic of Referensspelet, with each representing a different action the player can take while in a dungeon. They are colored based on which type of card they are, with teal being characters, red being attacks, blue being defenses, yellow being stat boosts, purple being mana generators, white being wild cards, and black being temporary cards.

## Damage calculation

Damage=(CardDamage+CardDamage*Combo+BaseDamage*Amount)*(1+Might)*(1+Area/5)*GemMultiplier1*GemMultiplier2

Damage: the number displayed on enemies.

CardDamage: the number displayed on cards while not in combat.

Combo: the multiplier number when stacking combos.

Amount: capped at 50.

Might: capped at 1000%.

Area: capped at 1000%. Every 5% area adds 1% bonus damage to every enemy in front.

GemMultiplier: Double Damage and Triple Damage have this. Stack multiplicatively.

## Sorting

As of 1.5, players can now sort their deck in two ways:

- By Mana: Cards, from left to right, will be listed in descending Mana cost order, then wild cards will be listed to the far right. For example, if among the cards in hand, the highest Mana cost is 3, cards with that Mana cost will be listed first, then ones with a Mana cost of 2 next to them, and so on. If the player has any wild cards, those will be listed next to the card(s) with the lowest Mana cost.
- By Color: From left to right, cards are sorted this way: attack cards (red, including evolved weapon cards), stat boost cards (yellow), defense cards (blue), mana cards (purple), crawler cards (green), wild cards (gray).

If the player receives a new card in hand, it will by unsorted by default. Furthermore, once the player receives a batch of cards after the enemies' turn, the batch of cards will be unsorted.
