# Crawlers:Outhouse

From Referensserien Wiki

[Jump to navigation](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Outhouse#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Outhouse#searchInput)

**Outhouse** is a [dungeon event](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Dungeon_events "Crawlers:Dungeon events") in _[Referensspelet](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors "Referensspelet: The Turbo Wildcard from Referensserien")_.

As the title suggest, this is but a simple medieval fantasy outhouse. Or in modern a non-fantasy term, a toilet (but not a water closet (WC), or could it be...).

Interacting with Outhouse (by "knocking" the door when walking into) allows recruiting a random unlocked / purchased [Crawler](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Characters "Crawlers:Characters") for a variable amount of gold (generally between 100 and 500) for the rest of the duration of the dungeon crawl run / session.

There is no upper limit / cap of how many crawlers one can have active during a dungeon crawl.
