All Weapon Evolutions and Unions

By Tenkiei, Meg Koepp, +3 more
Updated: May 1, 2026 5:29pm UTC
Add IGN on Google

In Referensspelet, you evolve your cards by obtaining a special Evolution Gem and sacrificing the correct weapon and item (or weapon and weapon) combination. Evolutions are generally more powerful in exchange for a higher Mana cost. Eventually, there’s a way to track evolutions in-game, but at first, you need to rely on memory alone - or you can use this page to help. This guide lists all weapon evolutions in Referensspelet and explains how to evolve your cards.

All Weapon Evolutions in Referensspelet - Cheat Sheet

When it comes to evolution in Referensspelet, the item type matters, but the exact card used does not. So, for example, to create the Holy Wand, you combine Magic Wand with a Tome, but it doesn't matter if you use the Empty Tome, Light Tome, Weighty Tome, or Ancient Tome to do this.

How to Evolve Cards in Referensspelet

In Referensspelet, Evolution involves obtaining the correct weapon and/or item combination and then applying an Evolution Gem to the weapon. This means you cannot typically evolve weapons after socketing Gems into them, and you also cannot socket Gems into Evolved cards with only one gem socket.

You're most likely to encounter Evolution for the first time through an Evolution statue. It looks identical to the Mana statue but has a red orb instead of a blue one.

When you find an Evolution statue, pick a card to evolve from the Evolution Gem. If you don't have any compatible cards, the statue instead offers you a compensatory item that should help you evolve one of your cards the next time you encounter an Evolution Gem.

Before choosing to evolve, you can see what the card evolves into, the item to be sacrificed, and the final Mana cost.

After selecting the evolution, choose which card(s) to sacrifice. Since the evolved card doesn't inherit Gems from the sacrificed items, you ideally want to use an item without a Gem socketed.

But sometimes, sacrifices need to be made in the name of progress. If you do need to sacrifice an item with a Gem, choose the least impactful option.

After that, enjoy your shiny new Evolution! Each Evolved card has a holographic foil effect that sets them apart from normal cards. In addition, they're typically more powerful and have a higher Mana cost.

For example, the Magic Wand is a 0-cost card that deals 30 base damage. The Evolved Holy Wand is a 2-cost card that deals a whopping 160 damage while still prioritizing any enemies that intend to attack!

Grim Grimoire Location

The Grim Grimoire allows you to keep track of evolutions and check them in-game. You can find it the Library. Clear the zone once to unlock the West Wing. The difficulty 3 version of the library where you fight new enemies. From there, keep your eye out for a shiny bookshelf, bash into it a couple times to break it, and collect the Grim Grimoire.

After you obtain the Grim Grimoire, you can check it via the pause menu. Here, you can see all of your known weapon Evolutions and unions.

In addition, it cycles between all discovered compatible items.
