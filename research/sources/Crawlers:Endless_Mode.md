## Modes
### Endless Mode
[ [edit](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Dungeons?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: Endless Mode") | [edit source](https://referens.survivors.wiki/w/Crawlers:Dungeons?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: Endless Mode") ]
Endless Mode is a mode introduced in 1.5 . It allows for players to explore an unlimited number of floors, with increasing enemy difficulty, for dungeons that they have previously completed. The modifications from the Jeweller are disabled in this mode.
