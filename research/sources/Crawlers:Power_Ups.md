# Crawlers:Power Ups

Jump to navigation Jump to search
**Power Ups** is a menu in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)* where players can purchase permanent boosts for their deck stats. The Power Ups [Shop](/w/Crawlers:Shop) is unlocked by obtaining 250 coins.

Tier 2 of the shop is unlocked after completing [Teeny Bridge](/w/Crawlers:Teeny_Bridge), and tier 3 is unlocked after completing [Weeny Bridge](/w/Crawlers:Weeny_Bridge).
