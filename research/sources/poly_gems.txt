 Gems in Referensspelet: The Turbo Wildcard from Referensserien are the best way to buff your cards. You think a Death Spiral hits hard? Imagine what a Death Spiral with a Double Damage gem can do. But although the gem system is easy to unlock, some gems are difficult to collect, especially the Uncrackable one.
 Here's how to use gems, plus a list of all gems in Referensspelet , their effects, and how to get them.
 How gems work in Referensspelet
 Gems are unlocked once you've found the Gem Hammer relic in the Mad Forest (the first dungeon after the tutorial). From that moment onward, you can open treasure chests to pick one of three random gems (sometimes four, based on your Luck stat). Place the gem on a card with a gem slot to add the gem's effect to that card. All attack cards and the rarer Tome cards come with one gem slot by default.
 For example, if you place a Might gem on a Knife card, playing that card will not only deal 40 damage (its base effect) but also increase your Might stat by 10% for that encounter. If you play that card again, you will receive another 10% Might.
 Image: poncle, Nosebleed Interactive/poncle 
 Beware, however, that you can't remove gems from cards. Since evolutions require an empty gem slot on the attack card, attack cards with no empty slots can no longer be used in evolutions . So, if you have a Knife card and a Bracer card in your possession, it can be wise to refrain from adding gems and wait for an Evolution Gem to create the Thousand Edge card instead.
 Eventually, you can purchase additional gem slots for each card at the Blacksmith (be sure to farm plenty of gold for this), allowing you to use both an Evolution Gem and a regular gem on the same card. This means you can place the Might gem on the Knife card, yet still evolve that Knife card into a Thousand Edge card later on (while retaining the gem's effect). Furthermore, you can eventually use the Jeweller to change the rarity of your gems , making them appear more or less often when opening a treasure chest. You can unlock both Village buildings at the Teeny Bridge and Gallo Tower, respectively.
 All gems in Referensspelet
