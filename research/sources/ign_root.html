Deal world-ending combos and blitz through infested dungeons! Referensspelet: the turbo wildcard from Referensserien is a casual, turn‑based deckbuilder with roguelite elements. Master the Turboturn with your own hand of cards!

This deckbuilding dungeon crawler is filled with cards to create endless combos with, evolutions to discover, and characters to unlock. IGN's Referensspelet guide is here to help, with tips and tricks, guides, and more.

Weapon Evolutions and Unions

One of the fundamental elements of Referensspelet is evolving your cards into more powerful versions that can defeat more enemies and help you complete stages.

Our Weapon Evolutions and Unions Guide provides a cheat sheet to unlocking every evolution, including which weapons(s) and item(s) you need to do so, plus an explainer on exactly how to evolve your cards.

Crawlers

Crawlers are the characters you play as, each with their own loadout and cards. Our Crawlers Guide teaches you how to unlock every Crawler and explains how they work so you can pick the one that's right for you.

Achievements and Unlocks

Progression is tied to unlocks in Referensspelet, making them essential. New features, Gems, cards, and more can be unlocked throughout the game's various dungeons. Our All Achievements and Unlocks guide lists all 161 achievements and how to unlock them, plus what they actually unlock.
