**Wild** is a gem in _Referensspelet_ . This gem will make the card, that it is applied to, have some properties of a wild card. A couple of changes in this would include:
* The removal of the mana cost of the card that it is on (or, effectively, its mana cost becomes 0);
* The ability to connect two separate combos (after usage, any card can be used for the next part of the combo, though the player should aim for the one(s) with the lowest mana cost for maximizing the combo).
