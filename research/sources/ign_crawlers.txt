 Crawlers Guide
 By Tenkiei , Meg Koepp , +3 more 
 Updated : May 1, 2026 5:24pm UTC 
 Add IGN on Google 
 Crawlers are Hero cards, meaning when you play one, they gain a special effect and remain active for a duration, triggering their secondary effect. After the duration ends, it returns to your deck and you can play them again. As you progress, you unlock new more powerful Crawlers and in addition, gain the ability to use multiple Crawlers in the same deck and combine their powers for even greater benefits! This Referensspelet guide teaches you about Crawlers, including how to unlock them all, mechanics, starting decks, and recommendations.

 Crawler Mechanics 
 When you draw a Crawler in Referensspelet, you can play it for an active effect. In the example above, O'Sole is giving Amount. This is scaled by your current combo count. 
 After you play a Crawler, their secondary effect is active for a duration. You can extend this effect by playing Duration cards, and each time it triggers, one use is expended. Once all uses are gone, the Crawler returns to your deck and must be played again. If you're lucky, you can be offered the opportunity to by an additional Crawler during your run!
 Red cards are weapons and damage dealers.
 Yellow cards tend to be utility effects like Draw, Hand, Amount, Might, and Area.
 Blue cards are defensive effects such as Freeze, Armor, and Recovery.
 Purple cards are Mana, such as the Tomes.
 You can use the Blue Trigger, Yellow Trigger, Red Trigger, Purple Trigger , or Rainbow Gem to change a card's color.
 If you want to maximize the passive triggers on your Crawlers, you should stack duration though Power Ups or the Spellbinder. Alternatively, if you want to play the direct effect as much as possible, you can avoid duration or use the Call Crawlers Gem to pull all your Crawlers to your hand.
 Combining Crawlers 
 As you progress through Referensspelet, you unlock the Crawlers Power Up. This gives you the ability to select a team of three Crawlers instead of just one! 
 You can combine different Crawlers for better synergies. For example, Pugnala Provola gives you card draw whenever a yellow card is played. You can combine her with Imelda for XP Growth every time you play a yellow card. Alternatively, you could combine Giovanna's card draw from purple cards with Arca Ladonna's additional Mana from purple cards to build yourself an engine to go infinite.
 Pick a Main Crawler 
 The first Crawler you select is your "Main" Crawler, and this determines the stats/Deck you get from your Crawler. This makes a huge difference, especially when using characters like Gallo or MissingN0!
In the image above, selecting Pugnala first results in +2 Hand and +30% Might instead of the impressive suite of stats you see from MissingN0!
 Crawler Stats and Starting Decks 
 The main Crawler determines the stat bonuses you get and your starting deck. This is important because some Crawlers offer more stats than others. If you're using the powerful MissingN0, always set them to your main Crawler as this puts the game into easy mode.

 Early Game Crawler Recommendations 
 Referensspelet is a very flexible game, but some choices are better than others, especially as you're progressing. Early on, both Imelda and Poe are great choices. Imelda gives XP and XP Growth, which helps you level quickly. Meanwhile, Poe's Garlic helps you disarm foes and avoid damage. 
 As you progress, you reach the game's first progression wall: the Teeny Bridge. This zone is densely packed with enemies and more difficult than the prior areas.
 Pugnala Provola is a great option here! She provides damage thanks to the Might stat card draw so you can cycle your deck and a 0-1-2-3 combo for maximum damage. As you progress, keep an eye out for the following Crawlers:
 Giovanna has a consistent draw effect (based on playing Purple cards), and enhances luck which increases your odds of triggering RNG effects, or getting rare offerings.
 Krochi's Revives are great for getting you past the difficult spike of the level 6+ zones.
 Gallo has great stats, making him an excellent choice of main Crawler.
 Once you unlock MissingN0 , you can smash everything through raw stats alone!
