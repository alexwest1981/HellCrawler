# Crawlers:Gems

Jump to navigation Jump to search
[![](/images/thumb/Crawlers_gem_hammer_tutorial.png/300px-Crawlers_gem_hammer_tutorial.png?67b7b)](/w/File:Crawlers_gem_hammer_tutorial.png)
**Gems** in *[Referensspelet](/w/Referens_Crawlers:_The_Turbo_Wildcard_from_Referens_Survivors)* are loot from level ups treasure chests found in [dungeons](/w/Crawlers:Dungeons). After the [![](/images/CSprite-Gem_Hammer.png?a4216)](/w/Crawlers:Gem_Hammer) [Gem Hammer](/w/Crawlers:Gem_Hammer) relic is acquired, it can be used to apply gems to [cards](/w/Crawlers:Cards), adding the gem's effect to the card.

Adding the same gem twice on a card will add the gem's effects twice (e.g. 10% Growth + 10% Growth will give 20% Growth when used). There is currently a bug where the card's description does not update to reflect the 2 gems.

The rarity of a gem depends on its individual rarity and the source. In the case of level ups, [![](/images/CSprite-Luck.png?bfcdd)](/w/Crawlers:Luck) [Luck](/w/Crawlers:Luck) will also play a role.

Gem rarities can be tweaked in the [Jeweller](/w/Crawlers:Jeweller) menu. However, when [Endless Mode](/w/Crawlers:Endless_Mode) is enabled, all gems will drop at their default rarity, regardless if their rarity has been tweaked or if they have been sealed.

Gems have a chance to be awarded in level ups, with Ultra Rare gems being the exception. Uncommon, Rare, and Very Rare gems will appear more frequently with higher Luck, with Luck benefitting the higher rarities more.

| Rarity | Weight | Luck Factor |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 60 | 0 |
| Uncommon | 30 | 0.035 |
| Rare | 20 | 0.065 |
| Very Rare | 6 | 0.3 |
| Ultra Rare | 0 | 0 |
Each type of treasure chest has its own set of rarities that can be awarded. Luck does not affect this distribution, unlike level ups. Instead, there is a luck-based chance for chests to award 4 gems instead of 3.

- If the player can [evolve](/w/Crawlers:Evolution) a weapon, then the Evolution gem may appear more frequently. Treasure chests are also able to provide multiple Evolution gems within a selection.
In the [Jeweller](/w/Crawlers:Jeweller) menu, when editing the rarity of Gems, the chests that gem can appear in will be showcased on the right. Chests that are faded out will not contain that gem. When a gem's rarity is increased, it will be removed from its original chest, making it only appear in higher-tier chests. When a gem's rarity is lowered, it will be just be added to lower tier chests, rather than being removed from its original.

| Rarity | Treasure Chest Type(s) Found In | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Tier 1 | 160 |
| Uncommon | Tier 1 | 120 |
| Rare | Tier 1 and Tier 2 | 80 |
| Very Rare | Tier 2 and Tier 3 | 60 |
| Ultra Rare | Tier 2 and Tier 3 | 40 |

- Tier 1 treasure chests are the normal chests found commonly within dungeons. These only reward Common, Uncommon, and Rare gems.
- Tier 2 treasures are the rare, locked, purple chests where the player must sacrifice cards in order to open them. These mostly reward Rare and Very Rare gems with a chance to reward Ultra Rare gems.
- Lastly, tier 3 chests are awarded by defeating bosses (at floor exit / descend, coffins, optional floor boss, and the lone ambush boss). These chests are ornately decorated clad in translucent gold and placed in the middle / center of pathways. These primarily reward Very Rare and Ultra Rare.
